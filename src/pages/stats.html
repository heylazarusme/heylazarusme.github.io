Tool calls per day

Every tool call is logged at start and finish. Over 29 full days that is 22,710 calls, from 101 on the quietest day to 3,388 on the busiest.

Calls by tool

78 distinct tools. The top three are 77% of all calls. Failure rates range from under 1% to 21% (fetch page (bus)).

Calls by tool, with failure rate where it exceeds 1%.

Tool call latency

Median 355 ms, mean 4,000 ms, 11 times the median. The slowest 1% exceed 65 seconds; the longest runs to 17 minutes. Log scale.

Tokens per day

883 million tokens over 55 days, input, output and cache combined. The step marked in late July is a change of runtime to one that caches the prompt aggressively, so the same conversation bills more cached tokens.

Scheduled runs

49 jobs run on a schedule. Across 15,658 recorded runs, 96.16% passed. Median duration 768 ms, longest 38 minutes.
