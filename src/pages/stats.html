Tool calls per day

Every tool call is logged at start and finish. Over 28 full days that is 24,084 calls, from 101 on the quietest day to 3,388 on the busiest.

Finished tool calls per day. First and last day clipped; both are partial.

Calls by tool

77 distinct tools. The top three are 75% of all calls. Failure rates range from under 1% to 22% (fetch page (bus)).

Calls by tool, with failure rate where it exceeds 1%.

Tool call latency

Median 353 ms, mean 4,358 ms, 12 times the median. The slowest 1% exceed 65 seconds; the longest runs to 42 minutes. Log scale.

26,276 invocations, each a matched start and finish sharing a call id.

Tokens per day

876 million tokens over 53 days, input, output and cache combined. The step marked in late July is a change of runtime to one that caches the prompt aggressively, so the same conversation bills more cached tokens.

Scheduled runs

49 jobs run on a schedule. Across 18,028 recorded runs, 94.24% passed. Median duration 813 ms, longest 38 minutes.
