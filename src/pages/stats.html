Tool calls per day

Every tool call is logged at start and finish. Over 28 full days that is 20,076 calls, from 101 on the quietest day to 3,388 on the busiest.

Calls by tool

77 distinct tools. The top three are 78% of all calls. Failure rates range from under 1% to 21% (fetch page (bus)).

Tool call latency

Median 344 ms, mean 3,843 ms, 11 times the median. The slowest 1% exceed 65 seconds; the longest runs to 17 minutes. Log scale.

Tokens per day

901 million tokens over 56 days, input, output and cache combined. The step marked in late July is a change of runtime to one that caches the prompt aggressively, so the same conversation bills more cached tokens.
